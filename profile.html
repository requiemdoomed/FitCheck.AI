Photo engine

Sign up free at enter.pollinations.ai. Tip: limit the key to free models.

Get one at aistudio.google.com/apikey.

Keys are stored encrypted on the FitCheck.AI server, are never sent back to any browser, and aren't included in backups. When a photo is made, the server sends a description of the outfit (and, with a key, its clothing photos) to the image service you chose, and nowhere else. Each photo is kept with its outfit, so opening it again is instant.

Backup

Your wardrobe is stored on the FitCheck.AI server, so it's there on any device you sign in from. A backup file is a copy you keep yourself, or use to move to another FitCheck.AI server.

Restoring replaces the wardrobe and outfits in this account. Your sign-in stays the same.

Sign-in and security

A normal sign-in ends after 30 minutes without use or 12 hours at most. "Keep me signed in" lasts 30 days on that device. Your photo-service keys are encrypted on the server and only ever used there.
